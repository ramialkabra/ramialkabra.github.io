Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

I'm Rami Al-Kabra, a real estate broker with eXp Realty in Bothell, WA. This page covers my Site Lines newsletter and its website (newsletter.ramialkabra.com), the app I use to post on Facebook and Instagram, and the information you share with me when we work together.

What I collect

  • Newsletter: your email address, plus your first and last name if you share them. Some readers I added from my own contacts. My newsletter service records whether you open an email and which links you click.
  • My website: a few cookies that count visits, remember your language, and tell real visitors apart from spam bots.
  • Facebook and Instagram: my posting app, "Rami Al-Kabra Publisher," reads the posts on my own Page and Instagram account, how they perform, and the public comments people leave on them, including the commenter's name as shown there. It never asks you to log in, and it reaches nothing outside my own accounts.
  • Working with me: your name, phone number, email, and what you're looking to buy or sell, kept in my client records.

How I use it

To send the newsletter, learn which topics help readers most, publish my posts, moderate comments (such as hiding abusive ones), and help you buy or sell a home.

Who else sees it

Only the companies that run these tools for me. beehiiv sends the newsletter and hosts my website, and Meta runs Facebook and Instagram, each under its own privacy policy. BoldTrail, a real estate CRM, stores my client records for me. If we do a transaction together, eXp Realty receives the records the law requires, and I'll connect you with a lender, title or escrow company only when you ask. I will never sell or rent your information.

beehiiv's analytics and security tools set their own cookies. I don't run ads on my website or in the newsletter today; if that changes, I'll update this page before the first ad runs.

Texts

If you give me your cell number, I'll text you about your search, your sale, or something you asked me about. I'll only text you about real estate you didn't ask about if you've said yes first. Reply STOP, or just tell me, and I'll stop. I never share your number or your permission to text with anyone else for their marketing. Message and data rates may apply.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter anytime with the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Email me at rami@ramialkabra.com to see, correct or delete what I have about you, including any copy of a comment you left on my posts. Some transaction records I'm required to keep.
  • My website doesn't respond differently to "Do Not Track" browser signals.

Kids

My website and newsletter aren't meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If I change this policy in a way that matters, I'll update the date above and mention it in the next newsletter.
